Please refine ONLY the way the Weekly Digest missionary items expand and collapse.

Do NOT redesign the existing page, sidebar, header, navigation, typography, colors, spacing, buttons, or overall SentConnect visual language. The current overall design and consistency are good and should remain intact.

The goal is to make the Weekly Digest lineup feel more polished, modern, and intentional while keeping the current design.

### Desired behavior and styling

Use the current layout as the foundation:

- One missionary can be expanded at a time.
- Expanded missionary shows the full update/content underneath the missionary header.
- Other missionaries remain collapsed as compact rows.
- Clicking the missionary row or chevron should smoothly expand/collapse it.
- Keep the existing information and controls exactly as they are.

### Visual refinement

For the COLLAPSED missionary rows:

- Keep them clean and lightweight.
- Do not use the current heavy solid-blue header treatment.
- Use the existing white/light background style of the page.
- Use subtle separation rather than strong borders.
- Keep the missionary avatar, name, date range, location, update count, Share button, menu, and chevron.
- The update-count pill can retain its existing light-blue treatment.
- Keep the small colored accent on the left side of each missionary row, but make it subtle.
- Avoid thick borders, heavy shadows, or overly bold styling.
- The collapsed rows should feel like part of one continuous Missions Feed rather than separate large accordion boxes.

For the EXPANDED missionary:

- Keep the existing expanded content and functionality.
- Make the transition from collapsed → expanded feel smooth and natural.
- The missionary header should remain visually connected to the content below it.
- Use a subtle blue accent to indicate the active/expanded missionary.
- Avoid making the entire header bright blue.
- Keep the post content, photos, reactions, comments, and sharing controls unchanged.
- The expanded section can have a very subtle elevation/shadow so it is clear that it is active.

### Interaction

Add a polished, subtle transition when opening and closing a missionary:

- Smooth height/opacity transition.
- Chevron rotates naturally between expanded and collapsed states.
- Avoid flashy animations.
- The interaction should feel fast and professional.

### Important design principle

The Weekly Digest should NOT look like a traditional accordion.

It should feel more like a modern mission communication feed where:

COLLAPSED = lightweight weekly summary

EXPANDED = the selected missionary's active feed

The hierarchy should come primarily from spacing, subtle background changes, the left accent, and the expanded content—not heavy borders or large blocks of color.

Please preserve the current SentConnect design and make ONLY these Weekly Digest expand/collapse refinements.

Before changing anything else, inspect the existing implementation and reuse the current components, styles, colors, and responsive behavior wherever possible.
