---
name: Weekly digest reminders
description: Product decision for the weekly administrator email and its timing.
---

Weekly email is a concise administrator reminder, not a copy of the report. Keep full post text, photos, and share tools inside the authenticated Weekly Digest tab. Send the reminder Friday morning in the `America/Los_Angeles` timezone and link directly to the admin digest view.

**Why:** The user explicitly wants administrators prompted to review the report inside SentConnect instead of receiving the full digest by email. Friday Pacific timing is the chosen weekly cadence.

**How to apply:** Future email changes must preserve the reminder-only model, privacy boundary, Friday Pacific schedule, and direct link to the Weekly Digest tab unless the user explicitly changes this decision.
